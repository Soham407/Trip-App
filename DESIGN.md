# Family Trip Expense & List App

## Design Direction

The interface should feel like a practical travel notebook, not a finance dashboard. It needs warmth, clarity, and enough structure that a family can use it in a car, hotel room, or at a roadside stop without friction.

## Visual Mood

- Light, tactile surfaces with tinted neutrals
- Strong contrast for fast reading outdoors and indoors
- Friendly category colors that stay organized rather than loud
- Utility-first layouts with one or two memorable moments, not decorative clutter

## Color Strategy

Committed palette, centered on warm paper neutrals and a deep ink base.

- Background: soft paper or sand tint
- Text: deep ink, not pure black
- Accent: terracotta or coral for trip-critical actions
- Secondary actions: moss or teal
- Warnings and pending states: saffron or amber

Avoid pure white, pure black, neon gradients, and glassy panels.

## Typography

- Use a distinctive rounded sans for body copy
- Use a more characterful display face only for section headers or the trip title
- Keep hierarchy obvious through size and weight, not decoration

## Layout Principles

- Make the current trip visible immediately
- Keep the needs-review queue above the fold
- Use tabs where the user already thinks in modes, especially Shopping vs Packing
- Prefer direct inline actions over modal-heavy flows
- Use cards sparingly, only where they help scanability

## Motion

- Subtle transitions only
- Fast easing, no bounce
- Use motion to confirm state changes, not to entertain

## Prototype Tone

The prototype should read as collaborative and dependable. It should feel like a family tool that works on the road, not a startup demo.
